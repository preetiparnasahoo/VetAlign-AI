# Demo video script — target 5:00 (fits both "3–5" and "5–10 minutes" in the handbook)

One take, camera on, screen recording of the **live public URL** (not localhost).
Dummy data only. Rehearse once. Speak to the camera, not the script; the bullets are prompts.

**Before recording:** open the live app in a fresh tab, select **Gemini 3.8 Flash**, keep
`test_audio/test1_hindi_stores_transport.wav` ready (or record live on the microphone),
close other tabs, silence notifications.

---

## 0:00–0:30 · Intro and problem (camera)
- "I'm [name], [one line about you]. This is VetAlign-AI."
- "A Havildar retiring after 15 years ran a battalion's stores and transport and trained 40 jawans.
  On a job portal that reads as nothing. Recruiters search 'inventory management', not 'stores'."
- "And writing a CV means typing in English. Many of these men would rather just *talk*."

## 0:30–0:50 · Who and what
- "It's for retiring soldiers, sailors and airmen, and the counsellors who help them."
- "You speak in Hindi, and you get a civilian profile plus three real roles you can apply to today."

## 0:50–2:30 · Live demo, voice in (screen)
1. Tick audio consent: "Consent comes before any audio leaves the browser."
2. Record or upload the Hindi note. Press **Transcribe**.
3. Point at the fields: "Army, Havildar, **15 years**. And notice it did **not** put the 40 jawans into
   years. Education is blank because I never said it. It doesn't invent."
4. "The veteran checks and corrects this, then approves." Tick approve and consent, then **Analyse**.
   *(While it runs, say what's happening: "Gemini is translating military duties into civilian skills
   and ranking open roles by skill.")*

## 2:30–3:40 · The output (screen)
- Read the three cards: title, employer, city, **why it fits**, **to check**.
- "Matched on skills, not titles. Employer, link and contact come from the job list, never from the AI,
  and every job here is clearly marked fictional."
- Open **Source facts**: "every skill traces back to something he actually said."
- Step 3: show the editable profile, download **Profile + matched roles**.

## 3:40–4:20 · Where AI does the heavy lifting (screen or camera)
- "Three things a form can't do: understand Hindi speech and tell years from team size; translate
  'convoy' into 'fleet coordination'; and judge fit by skills."
- "And three things I don't trust the AI with: every skill must cite his words, invented or closed jobs
  are thrown out in code, and a defence licence is never passed off as a civilian one."
- Optional, 10 seconds: "If Google's model is busy, it retries and switches to a backup, and tells you."

## 4:20–4:45 · What breaks (camera)
- "The job list is small, 13 fictional roles. The plan is for an ex-servicemen association volunteer
  to update it weekly. There's a one-command check for their edits, and stale roles hide themselves."
- [If tested with a real person: "I tested it with … and they said …" for the +5 bonus.]

## 4:45–5:00 · Impact line (camera)
- "[FILL with your measured number, e.g. 'A 30-second voice note became a reviewed profile and three
  matched roles in about two minutes, in Hindi, at zero cost.']"
- "From service experience to a civilian career. Thank you."
